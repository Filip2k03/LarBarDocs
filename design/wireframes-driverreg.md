# DriverReg Application Wireframes & UX Flow

DriverReg is a staff-assisted KYC application. It separates sensitive driver onboarding from the operational Driver App and creates a traceable review case for every registration.

## Prototype

- [Launch interactive DriverReg prototype](/prototypes/driverreg-app.html)
- [Open Figma-importable master canvas](/wireframes/labar_master_figma_canvas_v2.svg)
- Licence scan reference: `/idscan/drivinglicencesample.jpeg`

## Screen set

1. Staff MFA sign-in and device/branch binding
2. Driver consent and case creation
3. Personal details with DOB-derived age
4. NRC front/back capture and structured parsing
5. Driving licence camera scan
6. OCR autofill review with per-field confidence
7. Selfie liveness and face comparison
8. Vehicle and commercial compliance details
9. Review, declaration, and submission
10. Review status, correction, approval, and Driver App activation

The sample licence is a demonstration asset. Production prototypes must use synthetic or consented test identities and must never expose real NRC/licence data in public test links.
